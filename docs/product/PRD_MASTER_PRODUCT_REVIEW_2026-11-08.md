# Retained master product contracts 7–38 — 8 November 2026

This is a semantic review of chapters 7–38 of the frozen
[master PRD](../archive/history/PRD.md). It completes chapter mapping alongside
[foundations 1–6](PRD_MASTER_FOUNDATION_REVIEW_2026-11-08.md),
[contracts 39–51](PRD_MASTER_CONTRACT_REVIEW_2026-11-08.md) and the
[active delivery register](PRD_DELIVERY_REGISTER.v1.json). All four source
snapshots remain byte-identical. Mapping a chapter does not accept its features,
change commercial prices, modify contracts or authorize public deployment.

The user's current product direction is **agentic AI for business**, composed
across business models and hybrids. This review preserves customer journeys,
commercial tooling and shared platform commitments without replacing that
direction with an industry label or with the archive's employee wording.
